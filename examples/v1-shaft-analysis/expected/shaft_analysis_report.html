Assumptions and Limitations

Transmitted torque from power and rotational speed

  • Power and speed describe the same shaft operating point.
  • Steady mechanical power transmission.
  • Input power is non-negative and speed is positive.
  • The 9550 coefficient is an engineering approximation, not an exact conversion constant.
  • Does not calculate losses, efficiency, transient torque, or load variation.

Simply supported shaft or beam with same-direction transverse point loads

  • Two ideal simple supports at x = 0 and x = L.
  • One plane and static loading.
  • Non-negative same-direction transverse point loads.
  • Loads lie on or between the supports; coincident loads remain separate inputs.
  • No signed or opposite-direction loads, distributed loads, applied couples, overhung loads, two-plane loading, or dynamics.
  • No deflection or bearing-stiffness calculation.
  • Shaft self-weight is excluded unless explicitly represented as point loads; this approximation is not recommended by this model.
  • Not a general beam or shaft solver.

Theoretical solid-shaft diameter under combined bending and torsion

  • solid circular shaft
  • elastic stress analysis
  • steady bending moment and torque
  • theoretical strength sizing only
  • no fatigue or alternating loading
  • no shock or dynamic loading
  • no keyway, shoulders, or stress concentration
  • no axial force
  • no stiffness, deflection, or critical speed criterion
  • no standard preferred diameter
  • Theoretical minimum strength diameter only; not a final production diameter.
  • Does not evaluate fatigue, alternating or shock loading, keyways, shoulders, stress concentrations, or axial force.
  • Does not check stiffness, deflection, critical speed, or standard preferred diameters; the reference's Section 10.6 calls for further design checks.

The reported diameter is a theoretical minimum under the implemented model, not a final production shaft diameter.
